## Discretion Decisions Pinned In-Plan

These are the agent-discretion areas from CONTEXT/RESEARCH locked here with rationale (do not re-open):

- **Entry-price derivation (A3):** entry = the **decision-bar close** (the last closed M15 bar's `close` at the decision bar). The limit-trigger (D-01) fires when a later M15 bar's **high/low trades through `entry`** in the trade direction (long: bar `low <= entry`; short: bar `high >= entry`). Rationale: "signal fires at the decision close" is the simplest, most honest D-01 reading; it keeps the chart's entry line and R:R meaningful.
- **LLM narrative timing (OQ2):** **eager at assembly** — `run_narrative_pipeline` runs once per assembled setup inside `assemble_setup`, and its `NarrativeResult` (verdict/confidence/agreement/reasoning/citations + `narrative_status`/`reason`) is persisted on the record. Rationale: the dashboard becomes a pure reader; offline/failed LLM renders the labeled `⌀` (AI-07).
- **Min-P(WIN) qualification (OQ3):** **persist all detector-passing candidates** — no engine-side hard minimum. Add a `setup_min_p_win` config knob defaulting to `0.0` (0 = no engine filter), so history is complete and provable; the dashboard's min-probability display slider is a separate view-time filter.
- **Live-vs-persisted MT5 health (OQ4):** health status is **last-persisted state** (checkpoint `last_success_at` freshness / stall detection from the SQLite meta store). The engine and dashboard stay MT5-free; a stale heartbeat degrades to the UI-SPEC disconnected copy.
- **Trigger window (OQ5):** a single global `setup_trigger_window_bars` default **8**, and the trigger tests **bar high/low crossing entry** (wick tolerance, matching the SMC wick-touch convention).
- **Setup store location (A1):** a dedup-on-`setup_id` Parquet file at `data/setups/setups.parquet`, written atomically (tmp + `os.replace`, keep="last"). The path is derived from `cfg.bars_dir.parent` under a resolve-under-root guard (mirrors `backtest.runner._output_dirs`, ASVS V4).
